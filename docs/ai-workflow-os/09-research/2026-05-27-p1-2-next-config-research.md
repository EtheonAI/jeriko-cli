## Implementation Impact

- Keep app `next.config.ts` files as thin composition files.
- Keep `@repo/next-config` as the baseline config package.
- Remove unused config env keys with no consumers.
- Do not move security, analytics, or notifications config into those packages until their own package phases.
- Keep `reactCompiler: true` because the apps already carry the required compiler plugin.

## Open Questions

- Should analytics rewrites and PostHog CSP hosts move to `@repo/analytics/next-config` in P5.3?
